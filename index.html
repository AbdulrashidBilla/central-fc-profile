History

Central Football Club was established in 2012. Its early identity was shaped by players who played on the local grounds and gradually created a recognizable Central style: competitive, physical, resilient and strongly connected to the people around the club.

The surviving photographs provide a visual record of that evolution. They show different generations in different kits, alongside supporters and community members, and preserve moments that would otherwise live only in memory.

The pioneers

Among the names associated with Central's formative years is Rashkano Billa, one of the club's first pioneer players. He became particularly known for his tough tackling style and his work as a defensive midfielder. Within the club's own historical memory, he is widely regarded as one of the standout defensive midfielders in Bute football history and as one of the defining midfielders of Central's story.

Harun

Central secured the signature of Harun from Dokono FC in 2016. He cemented his place in the club's folklore. In 2017, Central players paid tribute to him as he prepared to retire from playing football.

The tribute stands as one of the clearest reminders that a club's history is not only measured in results, but also in the people whose service becomes part of its identity.

The next generation

Central's player lineage continues to change. Among the newer names described in the archive are Kim, a recently joined attacking midfielder and veteran football figure from Garissa, and Elmi, a striker. The archive also records Jirow, a winger who later went on to join Al Ahly, alongside veteran figures and younger players who represent the club's changing football landscape.

The Al Ahly chapter

Central's archive records a quarter-final victory over Al Ahly, followed by a final in which Central lost 2–0. A photograph from the final captures Rashka challenging Al Ahly captain Mala.

The club today

The current generation is younger and energetic, carrying the same crest into a new era. Recent squad photographs show Central's continuing emphasis on youth, competition and the sense of togetherness that has marked the club since its earliest years.

That continuity is central to the club's identity: the pioneers establish the culture, the captains carry responsibility, the coaches shape the team, and the next generation inherits the badge.

Club colours and identity

Central's visual identity has evolved through different kit designs while retaining the club's recognizable blue, red and white palette. The modern crest places the initials FC and a football inside the shield, beneath a star and crown, with THE WALLS written across the lower part of the badge.

Legacy

Central FC's archive is ultimately a record of continuity. The photographs preserve not just line-ups, but friendships, leadership, rivalry, celebration, retirement and the passage from one generation to another.
